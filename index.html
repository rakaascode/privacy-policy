1. Introduction & Architecture

This Privacy Policy applies to the Wifora mobile application (hereinafter referred to as "the Application" or "Wifora"). Wifora is designed as an offline-first, local peer-to-peer (P2P) communication tool that allows devices connected to the same Wi-Fi network or local access point to discover each other and exchange messages.

Core Privacy Commitment: Wifora operates without any central server infrastructure. We do not own, operate, or connect to any remote database to process your chat data, profile information, or network activity.

2. Data We Collect & How It Is Processed

Because Wifora operates strictly on your local network, personal data collection by the developer is zero. However, to enable local discovery and chat features, the application processes the following information locally on your device:

  • Profile Information (Display Name): When you set up your user profile, you choose a display name. This name is broadcast strictly across your current local Wi-Fi network using Network Service Discovery (NSD) so other local peers can identify you in chat.
  • Local Messages & Chat Content: Messages sent in private or group ("Ruang Bersama") chats are transmitted directly between devices via standard UDP/TCP network sockets on the local network.
  • Network Identifiers (SSID & Local IP): The application reads the connected Wi-Fi Network Name (SSID) and local IP address to identify local network boundaries and group devices on the same Wi-Fi.

None of the above information is transmitted outside your local Wi-Fi environment or sent to the developer.

3. Android Runtime Permissions Rationale

To deliver local peer-to-peer functionality, Wifora requires specific system permissions on your Android device. Below is a transparent breakdown of why each permission is requested:

Permission Purpose & Usage
ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION Required by Android OS (Android 8.0 through Android 12) to read the Wi-Fi Network Name (SSID). Wifora uses SSID solely to verify if devices are on the same Wi-Fi. We do NOT track, log, or record your physical GPS location.
NEARBY_WIFI_DEVICES Required on Android 13+ (API level 33+) to perform Network Service Discovery (NSD) for discovering local peers without requesting physical location access (configured with neverForLocation flag).
POST_NOTIFICATIONS Used on Android 13+ to display local notifications when a new peer message arrives while the application is in the background.
CHANGE_WIFI_MULTICAST_STATE Required to enable UDP Multicast socket listeners so your device can participate in local group chat rooms on the shared Wi-Fi network.
ACCESS_WIFI_STATE &
ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE		 Used to verify active Wi-Fi network connectivity and establish local socket connections between peers.

4. Data Storage, Retention & Deletion

All data generated within Wifora resides locally on your Android device:

  • Local Storage: Your display name, active peers list, and local message history are stored in an internal SQLite/Room database located within the app's protected sandbox storage space.
  • Data Retention: Data remains on your device until you manually clear it or uninstall the application.
  • Data Deletion: You can completely remove all stored profile data and chat history at any time by navigating to your device's Settings > Apps > Wifora > Storage > Clear Data, or simply by uninstalling the application.

5. Third-Party Services & Data Sharing

Wifora values your privacy and independence. Therefore:

  • We do not integration any third-party analytics SDKs (such as Google Analytics, Firebase Analytics, or Mixpanel).
  • We do not include advertising SDKs (such as Google AdMob).
  • We do not sell, rent, trade, or share any user data with third-party data brokers or marketing companies under any circumstances.

6. Children's Privacy (COPPA & GDPR Compliance)

Wifora is designed for general audiences. Because our application does not collect, store, or transmit personal data to any external server, it is fully compliant with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13 (or 16 in certain jurisdictions).

7. Security & Local Encryption

Communication within Wifora takes place over your local Wi-Fi network. While data is bounded within your local Wi-Fi coverage area, local network security depends on the security of the Wi-Fi network you are connected to (e.g., WPA2/WPA3 password protection). We advise users to use secure, trusted Wi-Fi networks when communicating on local channels.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in app functionality or legal requirements. Any updates will be published on this page with a revised "Effective Date". We encourage users to review this Privacy Policy periodically for any updates.
